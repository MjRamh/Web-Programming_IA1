AI & The Future

AI Businesses of 2025 - My Reflection

Source: ClickUp Blog - AI Businesses of 2025 ↗

My favourite idea from the AI Businesses of 2025 list is AI-powered personalised education tutors. - My reflection, February 2026

The concept of using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to tailor curriculum, pacing and content to the individual learner rather than providing a single curriculum for every student is simple yet revolutionary. As a student enrolled in multiple rigorous modules, I can relate to this concept personally. Picture an AI Tutor who knows you are not as proficient in Recursion as you are in Databases; the AI Tutor will devise an alternate learning sequence to support your learning.

Application to My Life

I could utilise AI learning applications to augment my course work at UTech, filling any real-time gaps. This technology is particularly successful for students in Jamaica that may have limited access to tutors; it makes access to quality education available to students regardless of their location.

Ethical Concerns & My Solution

The main ethical issues involved with developing AI to assist educationally are Data Privacy (AI will need to collect a significant amount of learning data), Algorithmic Bias (if a model is trained using a limited demographic of learners, that model may not be able to successfully assist any other types of learners) and Reliance (by solely using AI to solve problems, students may not develop independent problem-solving skills). The suggested resolution to these three primary ethical issues includes open-source models trained on large datasets comprising diverse learners; strict policies to anonymise all collected student data; and the deliberate inclusion of an appropriate amount of "struggle time," so that the AI-tutor allows students to independently work through a problem before presenting hint(s).

Real-World App

Khan Academy's Khanmigo - an AI tutor built on GPT-4. It guides students through problems with Socratic questioning rather than giving direct answers. Visit Khan Academy Labs ↗
